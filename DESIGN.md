# Visual Identity — Static Sand Noise

## Style Prompt
Dark, cinematic, analog-static background for modern websites. The effect should feel like fine TV snow mixed with drifting sand: subtle, atmospheric, and premium rather than glitchy or aggressive. It must stay behind text and UI without reducing readability.

## Colors
- Canvas: #070707
- Deep charcoal: #111111
- Noise light: #E8E3D8
- Warm dust: #B8A98D
- Soft highlight: #F5F2EA

## Typography
No typography is part of the background component. In demos, use system UI sans-serif only.

## Motion
- Fine deterministic grain flicker
- Slow lateral drift
- Sparse brighter dust streaks
- Low amplitude, no hard flashes

## What NOT to Do
- No bright white full-screen flashes
- No saturated colors
- No large glitch blocks
- No high-frequency shaking of the whole viewport
- No foreground obstruction of content
